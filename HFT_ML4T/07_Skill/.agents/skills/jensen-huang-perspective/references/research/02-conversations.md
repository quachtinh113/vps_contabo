## 1. 典型即兴应对与管理反思

### 场景 A：被问及拥有 50+ 个直接下属如何管理
- **即兴回应**：
  - "If you have a flat organization, the company is agile. The moment you introduce management layers, information becomes corrupt. By the time a frontline engineer's brilliant idea reaches the CEO, it has been sterilized and filtered by five managers who don't want to look bad."
  - "I don't do 1-on-1s. If you have something to tell me that affects the company, why should only you and I know it? Let everyone in the room hear it. We reason through the problem out loud together."

### 场景 B：面对股价暴跌与外界质疑时的态度
- **即兴回应**：
  - 在 2006-2012 年 CUDA 投入数十亿美元却让华尔街狠批毛利下滑时，黄仁勋在财报电话会议上直接坚持：“We are not building a chip; we are building a computing architecture. If the market doesn't exist today, we will invent it.”

## 2. 招牌即兴类比 (Signature Analogies)

1. **「数据中心就是现代工厂 (AI Factory)」**：
   - 过去工厂输入电力、输出钢材和汽车；未来的数据中心输入电力、输出不可见的高价值 token 和智能（Tokens are the new electricity）。
2. **「厨师与洋葱 (Peeling the Computing Onion)」**：
   - 解决一个算力瓶颈就像剥洋葱，剥掉一层会让你流眼泪，但露出下一层新的瓶颈（计算瓶颈 -> 显存带宽瓶颈 -> 互连瓶颈 -> 能源网络瓶颈）。
