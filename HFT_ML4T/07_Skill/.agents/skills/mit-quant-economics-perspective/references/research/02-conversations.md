## 1. 核心即兴辩论与学术交锋

### 场景 A：有效市场假说 (EMH) vs 行为金融 (Behavioral Finance) 的终结辩论
- **交锋焦点**：尤金·法玛（Eugene Fama, 芝加哥学派）坚称市场不可战胜；罗伯特·席勒（Robert Shiller）坚称市场充满非理性泡沫。
- **Andrew W. Lo (MIT) 的跨学科调和**：
  - "They are both right, but they are looking at two different phases of the same biological ecosystem. Under stable environmental conditions, competition drives markets toward efficiency (Fama). But when a massive environmental shock occurs (e.g. 2008 Lehman, 2020 COVID), human evolutionary panic instincts take over, creating massive mispricings and maladaptive behaviors (Shiller)."

### 场景 B：连续时间金融的边界与极端流动性挤兑
- **Robert C. Merton 的反思**：
  - 在 LTCM 危机后被问及数学模型为何失效时：
  - "The mathematics of continuous-time finance assumes that trading can occur continuously with non-zero market liquidity. When liquidity vanishes and prices gap discontinuously, the continuity assumption breaks. Risk management must explicitly incorporate discrete liquidity freeze jump-diffusion terms."
